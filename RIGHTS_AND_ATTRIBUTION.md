# Rights and attribution

## Historical work

The underlying monograph is by **P. Ya. Ufimtsev** and was first published in
Russian in 1962. The English-language report was prepared for the Foreign
Technology Division, Air Force Systems Command, U.S. Air Force, under report
number **FTD-HC-23-259-71**.

The report's document-control page marks it **UNCLASSIFIED** and gives the
distribution statement **“Approved for public release; distribution
unlimited.”** The copy in this repository was obtained from the
[Internet Archive item](https://archive.org/details/ufintsev-method-of-edge-waves-in-the-physical-theory-of-diffraction).

That distribution statement supports public dissemination of the report, but
this repository does not treat it as a blanket copyright determination or claim
ownership of the original monograph. The original scan is provided for
historical preservation, scholarship, verification, and research. Any rights
that may subsist in the historical text remain with their respective holders.

## Reconstructed edition

The LaTeX files are a page-by-page scholarly reconstruction of the publicly
released report. They retain the original authorship, text, equations, captions,
and references while providing new typesetting and independently recreated
vector artwork. No endorsement by P. Ya. Ufimtsev, the U.S. Air Force, the U.S.
Government, Lockheed Martin, or the Internet Archive is implied.
