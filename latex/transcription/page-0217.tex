Another important feature of the scattering characteristic is that it satisfies the reciprocity principle---that is, it does not change its value with a mutual interchange of $\vartheta$ and $\vartheta_0$. One may also show that the vibrator does not radiate in the directions along its axis, and it does not scatter electromagnetic waves with glancing irradiation, that is,
\[F(0,\vartheta_0)=F(\pi,\vartheta_0)=F(\vartheta,0)=F(\vartheta,\pi)=0.\tag{32.06}\]
Furthermore, using representation (28.25) for the functions $\psi$ and $\psi_\pm$, we obtain the following expression for scattering characteristic (32.05) in the direction of the mirror-reflected ray $(\vartheta=\pi-\vartheta_0)$:
\[\begin{aligned}
F(\pi-\vartheta_0,\vartheta_0)={}&-\frac{kL}{2\ln\dfrac{2i}{\gamma ka\sin\vartheta_0}}\\
&+kL\frac{(\psi_+^0)^2E\left(2kL\sin^2\dfrac{\vartheta_0}2\right)+(\psi_-^0)^2E\left(2kL\cos^2\dfrac{\vartheta_0}2\right)}{4\left(\ln\dfrac{2i}{\gamma ka\sin\vartheta_0}\right)^2}\\
&+\frac{i}{\left(\sin\vartheta_0\ln\dfrac{2i}{\gamma ka\sin\vartheta_0}\right)^2}\Biggl\{\ln\frac{2i}{\gamma ka\sin\vartheta_0}-\frac12\\
&\quad+\left(\frac12\psi_+^0\cos^2\frac{\vartheta_0}2-\ln\frac{i}{\gamma ka\sin\dfrac{\vartheta_0}2}\right)\psi_+^0e^{ikL(1-\cos\vartheta_0)}\\
&\quad+\left(\frac12\psi_-^0\sin^2\frac{\vartheta_0}2-\ln\frac{i}{\gamma ka\cos\dfrac{\vartheta_0}2}\right)\psi_-^0e^{ikL(1+\cos\vartheta_0)}\\
&\quad-\frac{\ln\dfrac{i}{\gamma ka}}D e^{2ikL}\bigl[\psi_+^0\psi e^{ikL(1-\cos\vartheta_0)}-\psi_-^0\bigr]\psi_+^0\\
&\quad-\frac{\ln\dfrac{i}{\gamma ka}}D e^{2ikL}\bigl[\psi_-^0\psi e^{ikL(1+\cos\vartheta_0)}-\psi_+^0\bigr]\psi_-^0\Biggr\}.
\end{aligned}\tag{32.07}\]
With glancing irradiation of a vibrator, when $\vartheta_0=0$ or $\vartheta_0=\pi$, it follows from this that $F(\pi,0)=F(0,\pi)=0$.

Now assuming that $\vartheta_0=\frac\pi2$ in Equation (32.07), we obtain the relationship

F\left(\frac\pi2,\frac\pi2\right)={}&-\frac{kL}{2\ln\dfrac{2i}{\gamma ka}}+kL\frac{\bar\psi^2 E(kL)}{2\left(\ln\dfrac{2i}{\gamma ka}\right)^2}\\
&+\frac{i}{\left(\ln\dfrac{2i}{\gamma ka}\right)^2}\left[\ln\frac{2i}{\gamma ka}-\frac12+\left(\frac12\bar\psi-2\ln\frac{\sqrt2\,i}{\gamma ka}\right)\bar\psi e^{ikL}+\right.
\end{aligned}\]
