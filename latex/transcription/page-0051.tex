Representing Expression (5.01) in the form
\[\mathbf E=\mathbf E_0e^{ikz\cos\gamma-ik_1(x\cos\phi_0+y\sin\phi_0)}\tag{5.09}\]

For this purpose, it is sufficient to replace in Equations (4.05) and (4.06) $k$ by $k_1$, and $E_{0z}$ and $H_{0z}$ by $E_{0z}e^{ikz\cos\gamma}$ and $H_{0z}e^{ikz\cos\gamma}$. As a result, we obtain
\[\left.\begin{aligned}E_z=-H_\phi&=E_{0z}f^1(\phi,\phi_0)\frac{e^{i(k_1r+\frac\pi4)}}{\sqrt{2\pi k_1r}}e^{ikz\cos\gamma},\\H_z=E_\phi&=H_{0z}g^1(\phi,\phi_0)\frac{e^{i(k_1r+\frac\pi4)}}{\sqrt{2\pi k_1r}}e^{ikz\cos\gamma}.\end{aligned}\right\}\tag{5.10}\]
The angle $\phi_0$ introduced here is determined by the condition
\[e^{ik(x\cos\alpha+y\cos\beta)}=e^{-ik_1(x\cos\phi_0+y\sin\phi_0)},\tag{5.11}\]
hence
\[\operatorname{tg}\phi_0=\frac{\cos\beta}{\cos\alpha}.\tag{5.12}\]
The remaining components of the field created by the nonuniform part of the current with the oblique incidence of a plane wave are found from relationships (5.04), and when $kr\gg1$ they equal
\[\begin{aligned}E_r&=-\operatorname{ctg}\gamma E_z,&H_r&=-\operatorname{ctg}\gamma H_z,\\E_\phi&=\frac1{\sin\gamma}H_z,&H_\phi&=-\frac1{\sin\gamma}E_z.\end{aligned}\tag{5.13}\]
The equiphase surfaces for these waves have the form
\[r\sin\gamma+z\cos\gamma=\mathrm{const}\tag{5.14}\]
