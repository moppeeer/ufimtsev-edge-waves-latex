The coefficients $a_s$ and $b_s$ may be determined by means of Green's theorem
\[\oint_L\frac{\partial u}{\partial n}\,dl=\int\Delta u\,dS,\quad dS=r\,dr\,d\phi\tag{1.10}\]
for the contour $L$ in the plane $z=\mathrm{const}$ which is shown in Figure 1. Here, the external normal to the contour $L$ is designated by the letter $n$. Applying Equation (1.10) to the functions $A_z^e$ and $A_z^m$ and performing the limiting transitions $r_1\to r_0$ and $r_2\to r_0$ in it, we obtain
\[\begin{aligned}\int\left(\left.\frac{\partial A_z^e}{\partial r}\right|_{r_0+0}-\left.\frac{\partial A_z^e}{\partial r}\right|_{r_0-0}\right)r_0d\phi&=i\frac{4\pi k}{r_0}p_z\int\delta(\phi-\phi_0)r_0d\phi,\\\int\left(\left.\frac{\partial A_z^m}{\partial r}\right|_{r_0+0}-\left.\frac{\partial A_z^m}{\partial r}\right|_{r_0-0}\right)r_0d\phi&=i\frac{4\pi k}{r_0}m_z\int\delta(\phi-\phi_0)r_0d\phi.\end{aligned}\]
Since here the integration limits are arbitrary, it follows from the equality of the integrals that the integrands are equal:
\[\left.\frac{\partial A_z^e}{\partial r}\right|_{r_0+0}-\left.\frac{\partial A_z^e}{\partial r}\right|_{r_0-0}=i\frac{4\pi kp_z}{r_0}\delta(\phi-\phi_0),\tag{1.11}\]
\[\left.\frac{\partial A_z^m}{\partial r}\right|_{r_0+0}-\left.\frac{\partial A_z^m}{\partial r}\right|_{r_0-0}=i\frac{4\pi km_z}{r_0}\delta(\phi-\phi_0).\tag{1.12}\]
Now let us substitute Expressions (1.07) into Equality (1.11) and multiply both members of the latter by $\sin\nu_s\phi$. Then integrating the resulting equality over $\phi$ in the limits from $0$ to $\alpha$, we find
\[a_s=\frac{4\pi^2}{\alpha}kp_z.\tag{1.13}\]
In a similar way, let us determine the coefficients
\[b_s=\varepsilon_s\frac{4\pi^2}{\alpha}km_z,\tag{1.14}\]
where
\[\varepsilon_0=\frac12,\quad\varepsilon_1=\varepsilon_2=\cdots=1.\tag{1.15}\]
