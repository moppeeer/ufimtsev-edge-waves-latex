Now let us calculate the field arising during diffraction by a wedge of a plane wave (1.24). The field scattered by the first face $(\phi=0)$ is determined by the relationship
\[\left.\begin{aligned}H_z&=-\frac{\partial A_x}{\partial y},\\H_x&=H_y=0.\end{aligned}\right\}\tag{3.23}\]
One may write the component $H_z$ in the form
\[H_z=-\frac i2H_{0z}\frac\partial{\partial y}I_1\tag{3.24}\]
or
\[\left.\begin{aligned}H_z&=-\frac i2H_{0z}\cdot I_2\quad\text{with }\phi<\pi,\\H_z&=\frac i2H_{0z}\cdot I_2\quad\text{with }\phi>\pi.\end{aligned}\right\}\tag{3.25}\]
The quantity $I_2$ introduced here is the integral
\[I_2=\frac1\pi\int_{-\infty}^{\infty}\frac{e^{i(|y|v-wx)}}{k\cos\phi_0-w}\,dw\tag{3.26}\]
along the infinite contour which passes above the pole $w=k\cos\phi_0$. This integral, precisely the same as integral $I_1$, is transformed to an integral along the contour $D_0$. As a result, we obtain
\[\frac{H_z}{H_{0z}}=\begin{cases}v_2^+(\phi,\phi_0)+e^{-ikr\cos(\phi+\phi_0)}&\text{with }0<\phi<\pi-\phi_0,\\v_2^+(\phi,\phi_0)&\text{with }\pi-\phi_0<\phi<\pi,\\v_2^-(\phi,\phi_0)&\text{with }\pi<\phi<\pi+\phi_0,\\v_2^-(\phi,\phi_0)-e^{-ikr\cos(\phi-\phi_0)}&\text{with }\pi+\phi_0<\phi<\alpha,\end{cases}\tag{3.27}\]
where
\[v_2^{\pm}(\phi,\phi_0)=\mp\frac i{2\pi}\int_{D_0}\frac{\sin(\zeta\pm\phi)e^{ikr\cos\zeta}}{\cos\phi_0+\cos(\zeta\pm\phi)}\,d\zeta.\tag{3.28}\]
