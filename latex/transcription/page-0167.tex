Equations (24.20) and (24.21) have the following important properties, They do not have discontinuities, they include the case of glancing incidence of a plane wave, and they satisfy the reciprocity principle. From them it follows that $E_\vartheta=H_\varphi=0$ when $\vartheta=\pi/2$---that is, the fringing field does not experience a discontinuity on the plane $z=0$. Moreover, $E_\vartheta=H_\varphi=0$ with any values of $\vartheta$, if $\gamma=\pi/2$,---that is, a plane wave polarized perpendicular to the disk's plane does not experience diffraction with glancing irradiation of the disk.

As in the case of diffraction by a strip, the new approximation expressions consider to some extent tertiary diffraction [see Equations (23.05) and Figure 48].
