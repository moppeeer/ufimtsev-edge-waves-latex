Let us introduce the designation
\[\mathbf J=\int_0^\infty\mathbf j_1^1(\zeta)e^{\pm ik\zeta\cos\omega}d\zeta+\int_0^\infty\mathbf j_2^1(\zeta)e^{\mp ik\zeta\cos\Omega}d\zeta.\tag{16.09}\]
Equating Expressions (16.06) and (16.08), we find
\[J_{z_1}=\frac{cE_{0z_1}(\psi)}{ik2\pi}f^1,\quad J_{\phi_1}=\frac{cH_{0z_1}(\psi)}{ik2\pi}g^1.\tag{16.10}\]
The components $J_{z_1}$ and $J_{\phi_1}$ are mutually perpendicular, and when $\vartheta=0$ and $\vartheta=\pi$ they are parallel to the plane $xOy$ (Figure 30). The different orientation of the unit vector $\mathbf e_{\phi_1}$ when $\vartheta=0$ and $\vartheta=\pi$ is connected with the fact that the angle $\phi_1$ is measured from the irradiated face of the wedge. In the original $x$, $y$, $z$ coordinate system the vector $\mathbf J$ has the components
\[\left.\begin{aligned}J_x&=J_{z_1}\sin\psi-J_{\phi_1}\cos\psi,\\J_y&=-J_{z_1}\cos\psi-J_{\phi_1}\sin\psi\end{aligned}\right\}\quad\text{with }\vartheta=0\tag{16.11}\]
and
\[\left.\begin{aligned}J_x&=J_{z_1}\sin\psi+J_{\phi_1}\cos\psi,\\J_y&=-J_{z_1}\cos\psi+J_{\phi_1}\sin\psi\end{aligned}\right\}\quad\text{with }\vartheta=\pi.\tag{16.12}\]
Substituting Expressions (16.10) here, we obtain
\[\left.\begin{aligned}J_x&=\frac c{ik2\pi}[f^1E_{0z_1}(\psi)\sin\psi-g^1H_{0z_1}(\psi)\cos\psi],\\J_y&=-\frac c{ik2\pi}[f^1E_{0z_1}(\psi)\cos\psi+g^1H_{0z_1}(\psi)\sin\psi]\end{aligned}\right\}\ \text{with }\vartheta=0\tag{16.13}\]
and
\[\left.\begin{aligned}J_x&=\frac c{ik2\pi}[f^1E_{0z_1}(\psi)\sin\psi+g^1H_{0z_1}(\psi)\cos\psi],\\J_y&=-\frac c{ik2\pi}[f^1E_{0z_1}(\psi)\cos\psi-g^1H_{0z_1}(\psi)\sin\psi]\end{aligned}\right\}\ \text{with }\vartheta=\pi.\tag{16.14}\]
