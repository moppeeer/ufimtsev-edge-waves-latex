On the other hand, in accordance with \S\ 5 this field equals
\[
\left.\begin{aligned}
E_{x_2}&=e^{-ikx_1\cos\alpha_1}E_{0x_2}f^1(\varphi_2,\varphi_2^0)\frac{e^{i(k_1r_1+\pi/4)}}{\sqrt{2\pi k_1r_1}}e^{ik_1a(\sin\varphi_2^0-\sin\varphi_2)},\\
H_{x_2}&=e^{-ikx_1\cos\alpha_1}H_{0x_2}g^1(\varphi_2,\varphi_2^0)\frac{e^{i(k_1r_1+\pi/4)}}{\sqrt{2\pi k_1r_1}}e^{ik_1a(\sin\varphi_2^0-\sin\varphi_2)}.
\end{aligned}\right\}\tag{11.23}
\]
Here
\[
k_1(\sin\varphi_2-\sin\varphi_2^0)=-\sqrt{\lambda^2+\mu^2},\tag{11.24}
\]
and the functions $f^1(\varphi_2,\varphi_2^0)$ and $g^1(\varphi_2,\varphi_2^0)$ are determined by the equations:
\[
\left.\begin{aligned}
f^1(\varphi_2,\varphi_2^0)&=\frac{\sin\frac{\varphi_1-\varphi_1^0}{2}-\cos\frac{\varphi_1+\varphi_1^0}{2}}{\sin\varphi_1^0-\sin\varphi_1}+\frac{\cos\varphi_1^0}{\sin\varphi_1^0-\sin\varphi_1},\\
g^1(\varphi_2,\varphi_2^0)&=-\frac{\sin\frac{\varphi_1-\varphi_1^0}{2}+\cos\frac{\varphi_1+\varphi_1^0}{2}}{\sin\varphi_1^0-\sin\varphi_1}+\frac{\cos\varphi_1}{\sin\varphi_1^0-\sin\varphi_1}
\end{aligned}\right\}\tag{11.25}
\]

Equating the quantities (11.20) and (11.23), we find
\[
\left.\begin{aligned}
I_{y_2}(\psi_2)&=-\frac{c}{ik2\pi}\frac{e^{ika\sqrt{\lambda^2+\mu^2}}}{\sin\alpha_1\cos\varphi_1}H_{0x_2}g^1(\varphi_2,\varphi_2^0),\\
I_{x_2}(\psi_2)&=\frac{c}{ik2\pi}\frac{e^{ika\sqrt{\lambda^2+\mu^2}}}{\sin^2\alpha_1}\bigl[E_{0x_2}f^1(\varphi_2,\varphi_2^0)\\

\end{aligned}\right\}\tag{11.26}
\]
In this way we established the relationship between the nonuniform part of the current on the half-plane and its field in the far zone. Now let us return to a calculation of the field from the nonuniform part of the current flowing on the disk.
