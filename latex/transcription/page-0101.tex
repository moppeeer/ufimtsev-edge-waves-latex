Thus only the functions $f$ and $g$ are included in the final expressions for the scattering characteristic of a plane wave by a cylinder.

In the direction $\vartheta=\pi$, the functions $\overline\Sigma(\vartheta)$ and $\Sigma(\vartheta)$ take, as in the case of a disk, the values
\[\overline\Sigma(\pi)=-\Sigma(\pi)=kae^{-ikl},\tag{15.06}\]
and with $\vartheta=\frac\pi2$ they respectively equal
\[\left.\begin{aligned}
\overline\Sigma\left(\frac\pi2\right)&=-\left(\frac{\frac2n\sin\frac\pi n}{\cos\frac\pi n-1}+\frac1n\operatorname{ctg}\frac\pi n+ikl\right)[J_1(\zeta)-iJ_2(\zeta)],\\
\Sigma\left(\frac\pi2\right)&=i\left(\frac{\frac4n\sin\frac\pi n}{\cos\frac\pi n-1}-\frac1n\operatorname{ctg}\frac\pi n-ikl\right)J_2(\zeta)\\
&\quad+\left(\frac1n\operatorname{ctg}\frac\pi n+ikl\right)J_1(\zeta),
\end{aligned}\right\}\tag{15.07}\]
where $\zeta=2ka$. The terms in this equation which contain the factor $kl$ refer to the field from the uniform part of the current, and the remaining terms refer to the field from the nonuniform part of the current.
