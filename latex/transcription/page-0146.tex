The first terms in Expressions (22.06) and (22.07) are the desired secondary waves, and the last terms in the expressions are the fields from the current filaments located above the ideally conducting plane $x=0$ and having the moments
\[
p_{1z}^-=\frac{i}{4\pi k^3h}E_{0z}\widetilde f(1)e^{ika\sin\alpha},\qquad p_{2z}^-=\frac{i}{4\pi k^3h}E_{0z}\widetilde f(2)e^{-ika\sin\alpha}.\tag{22.08}
\]
where
\[
\widetilde f(1)=\frac{f(1)}{\cos\varphi}\bigg|_{\varphi=-\pi/2+0};\qquad\widetilde f(2)=\frac{f(2)}{\cos\varphi}\bigg|_{\varphi=\pi/2-0}.\tag{22.09}
\]
Summing the secondary waves which have been found with the unperturbed primary waves, we obtain the total field scattered by the strip
\[
\begin{aligned}E_z=-H_\varphi={}&E_{0z}\frac2\pi\frac{e^{ikr}}{\sqrt{2kr}}\Bigl\{\widetilde f(1)\Bigl[\cos\varphi\!\times\!\int_\infty^{2\sqrt{ka}\cos(\pi/4-\varphi/2)}e^{iq^2}\,dq\\
&+\frac{i}{2\sqrt{ka}}\sin(\pi/4-\varphi/2)e^{2ika(1+\sin\varphi)}\Bigr]e^{ika(\sin\alpha-\sin\varphi)}\\
&+\widetilde f(2)\Bigl[\cos\varphi\!\times\!\int_\infty^{2\sqrt{ka}\cos(\pi/4+\varphi/2)}e^{iq^2}\,dq\\
&+\frac{i}{2\sqrt{ka}}\sin(\pi/4+\varphi/2)e^{2ika(1-\sin\varphi)}\Bigr]e^{-ika(\sin\alpha-\sin\varphi)}\Bigr\}\\
&+E_{0z}[f(1)e^{ika(\sin\alpha-\sin\varphi)}+f(2)e^{-ika(\sin\alpha-\sin\varphi)}]\times\frac{e^{i(kr+\pi/4)}}{\sqrt{2\pi kr}}.
\end{aligned}\tag{22.10}
\]
Now assuming, as in the case of the H-polarization, that the moments $p_{1z}^{\pm}$ and $p_{2z}^{\pm}$ depend on the angle $\phi$, by replacing
\[
\widetilde f(1)\quad\text{by}\quad\frac{f(1)}{\cos\varphi}\qquad\text{and}\qquad\widetilde f(2)\quad\text{by}\quad\frac{f(2)}{\cos\varphi},\tag{22.11}
\]
we obtain
\[
\begin{aligned}E_z=-H_\varphi=E_{0z}\bigl[&F(1,\varphi)f(1)e^{ika(\sin\alpha-\sin\varphi)}\\&+F(2,\varphi)f(2)e^{-ika(\sin\alpha-\sin\varphi)}\bigr]\frac{e^{i(kr+\pi/4)}}{\sqrt{2\pi kr}}.
\end{aligned}\tag{22.12}
\]
