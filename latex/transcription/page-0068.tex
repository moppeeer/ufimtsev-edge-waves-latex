For the direction $\theta=0$, we have according to Equation (8.01)
\[\mathbf A=\frac ac\frac{e^{ikR}}R\int_0^{2\pi}d\psi\int_0^a\mathbf j^1(\rho,\psi)\,d\rho,\tag{8.14}\]
but in accordance with equalities (8.09)--(8.11)
\[\left.\begin{aligned}\int_0^a j_{x_1}^1(\rho,\psi)\,d\rho&=\frac{ic}{4\pi k}H_{0x}\cos\psi,\\\int_0^a j_{y_1}^1(\rho,\psi)\,d\rho&=\frac{ic}{4\pi k}H_{0x}\sin\psi.\end{aligned}\right\}\tag{8.15}\]
Consequently,
\[\left.\begin{aligned}A_x&=\frac ac\frac{e^{ikR}}R\left[\int_0^{2\pi}\cos\psi\,d\psi\int_0^a j_{y_1}^1(\rho,\psi)\,d\rho+\right.\\&\qquad\left.+\int_0^{2\pi}\sin\psi\,d\psi\int_0^a j_{x_1}^1(\rho,\psi)\,d\rho\right]=0,\\A_y&=\frac ac\frac{e^{ikR}}R\left[-\int_0^{2\pi}\cos\psi\,d\psi\int_0^a j_{x_1}^1(\rho,\psi)\,d\rho+\right.\\&\qquad\left.+\int_0^{2\pi}\sin\psi\,d\psi\int_0^a j_{y_1}^1(\rho,\psi)\,d\rho\right]=0,\end{aligned}\right\}\tag{8.16}\]
that is, in the direction of the main fringe $(\theta=0)$ the field from the nonuniform part of the current equals zero.
