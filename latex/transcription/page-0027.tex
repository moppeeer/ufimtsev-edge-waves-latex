With an arbitrary incidence of a plane wave on a wedge, one of two cases may occur: (1) the plane wave ``illuminates'' only one face of the wedge $(0<\phi_0<\alpha-\pi)$, and (2) the plane wave ``illuminates'' both faces of the wedge $(\alpha-\pi<\phi_0<\pi)$. Let us write out in more detail the functions $u(r,\psi)$ corresponding to these cases. In the case $\phi_0<\alpha-\pi$ (Figure 5), we have
\[\left.\begin{aligned}\left.\begin{aligned}u(r,\phi-\phi_0)&=v(r,\phi-\phi_0)+e^{-ikr\cos(\phi-\phi_0)}\\u(r,\phi+\phi_0)&=v(r,\phi+\phi_0)+e^{-ikr\cos(\phi+\phi_0)}\end{aligned}\right\}&\text{with }0<\phi<\pi-\phi_0,\\\left.\begin{aligned}u(r,\phi-\phi_0)&=v(r,\phi-\phi_0)+e^{-ikr\cos(\phi-\phi_0)}\\u(r,\phi+\phi_0)&=v(r,\phi+\phi_0)\end{aligned}\right\}&\text{with }\pi-\phi_0<\phi<\pi+\phi_0,\\\left.\begin{aligned}u(r,\phi-\phi_0)&=v(r,\phi-\phi_0)\\u(r,\phi+\phi_0)&=v(r,\phi+\phi_0)\end{aligned}\right\}&\text{with }\pi+\phi_0<\phi<\alpha,\end{aligned}\right\}\tag{1.31}\]
and in the case $\alpha-\pi<\phi_0<\pi$ (Figure 6) we have
\[\left.\begin{aligned}\left.\begin{aligned}u(r,\phi-\phi_0)&=v(r,\phi-\phi_0)+e^{-ikr\cos(\phi-\phi_0)}\\u(r,\phi+\phi_0)&=v(r,\phi+\phi_0)+e^{-ikr\cos(\phi+\phi_0)}\end{aligned}\right\}&\text{with }0<\phi<\pi-\phi_0,\\\left.\begin{aligned}u(r,\phi-\phi_0)&=v(r,\phi-\phi_0)+e^{-ikr\cos(\phi-\phi_0)}\\u(r,\phi+\phi_0)&=v(r,\phi+\phi_0)\end{aligned}\right\}&\text{with }\pi-\phi_0<\phi<2\alpha-\pi-\phi_0,\\\left.\begin{aligned}u(r,\phi-\phi_0)&=v(r,\phi-\phi_0)+e^{-ikr\cos(\phi-\phi_0)}\\u(r,\phi+\phi_0)&=v(r,\phi+\phi_0)+e^{-ikr\cos(2\alpha-\phi-\phi_0)}\end{aligned}\right\}&\text{with }2\alpha-\pi-\phi_0<\phi<\alpha,\end{aligned}\right\}\tag{1.32}\]
