\chaptertitle{VI}{CERTAIN PHENOMENA CONNECTED WITH THE NONUNIFORM PART OF THE SURFACE CURRENT}
\bigskip
In the previous chapters, a theoretical investigation was conducted of the field radiated by the nonuniform part of the current. In this chapter we will discuss a method for measuring this field (\S~26) and we will investigate the phenomenon of the reflected signal's depolarization (\S~27).

An experimental method for measuring the field from the nonuniform part of the current was first proposed for bodies of rotation in the paper of Ye. N. Mayzel's and the author [12]. Later it was shown that this method has a universal character, and is suitable for measuring the field from the nonuniform part of the current excited by a plane wave on any metal body [13].
